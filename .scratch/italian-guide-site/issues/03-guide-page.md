**What to build:** A beginner can read one ordered roadmap from zero to reading native Italian, and tick off each stage in a checklist at the end.

**Blocked by:** 01 (Home page and build test), 02 (Setup page)

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] The pronunciation and spelling section covers c and g before e and i, gl, gn, sc, double consonants, and the grave accent on stressed final vowels. It says that stress elsewhere is unmarked and when to check a dictionary.

- [ ] Sections follow on consuming native content, talking and writing to Italian speakers, and what comes next.
- [ ] The page ends with a checklist of every stage.
- [ ] Home links to the Guide where it fits.
