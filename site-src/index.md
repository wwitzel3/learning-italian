# Home

## What this site is

This site is a guide to learning Italian mostly by reading and listening to things Italians made for other Italians. You spend a short time on pronunciation and basic grammar, set up two tools, and then move into real Italian: series, films, books, podcasts, news, fumetti. Each unknown word you meet goes into a flashcard deck, and the deck grows out of what you actually read and watch.

The method is simple. You understand a language by hearing and reading a lot of it at a level you can mostly follow. Most of this site is about making that possible from the first months, when almost nothing is easy to follow yet.

## Who it's for

It's for English speakers who want to understand Italian as it's spoken and written, and who are willing to put in an hour or more a day for a year or longer. You don't need any Italian to start.

If you need to pass a school exam next month or want polite phrases for a holiday, a phrasebook or a course will serve you better. This guide is slow at first and fast later.

## How to use it

Read the pages in the order of the tabs across the top. The tabs run from the roadmap, through setup and day-to-day practice, to reference lists you'll return to for months.

You don't have to follow every recommendation. If a tool or a step doesn't suit you, skip it and keep the part that works. The one habit to keep is daily contact with Italian you find interesting.
